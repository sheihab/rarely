How to play

Rarely

  1. 7 prompts a day, like Name a fruit. 25 seconds each.
  2. Type one answer. Rarer answers score more: 10, 30, 60, 85 or 100.
  3. New prompts every midnight. Unlimited mode never ends.

Example: Name a fruit. apple 10 · mango 30 · papaya 60 · kumquat 85 · carambola 100.

Squarely

  1. Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries.
  2. Green: right letter, right spot. Gold: right letter, wrong spot.
  3. New word every day. Unlimited gives you a fresh word straight away.

Quickly

  1. Unscramble the letters into a word. 60 seconds total.
  2. 10 points per letter. Skip if stuck.
  3. Daily set for everyone, or unlimited.
