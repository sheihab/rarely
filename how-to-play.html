How to play Rarely

Rarely is a free daily word game. The twist: the rarer your word, the more points you score.

The rules

  1. Every day there are 7 prompts, the same for everyone, like "Starts with QU" or "Contains Z".
  2. For each prompt, type one real English word that fits.
  3. Your word scores 1–100 points depending on how rare it is in everyday English.
  4. Add up all 7 for your daily score, out of 700.
  5. A new puzzle arrives at midnight. Come back daily to build your streak.

Rarity tiers

Common 1–14Uncommon 15–34Rare 35–59Epic 60–84Legendary 85–100

Example

Prompt: Starts with QU. quiet scores 13 (common). quota scores 32 (uncommon). quiver scores 48 (rare). quiddity scores 96 (legendary).

Tips for high scores

  • Think of science, nature and old-fashioned words: animals, plants, minerals, instruments.
  • Longer words are often rarer, but not always.
  • Skip a prompt if you're stuck (0 points) rather than burning time.
  • After each answer we show the rarest possible word, so you learn as you play.

Other modes

Unlimited gives you endless random rounds. The Archive lets you play every past daily puzzle.
